# Image review, CAPTCHA and legal pages
- **Image review (`0013`).** Until you have automated scanning, posts with images wait in the admin "Image review" queue and appear only after a moderator approves them (platform_config `media_review_required`, default 1; set it to 0 to publish immediately). Authors see "it's with a moderator". Reject removes the post, and its images are deleted from storage by the media cron. This makes launch safer but needs moderators watching the queue; it does not replace Cloudflare's CSAM Scanning Tool or a child-safety procedure.
- **CAPTCHA.** Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY` (web and admin) and enable CAPTCHA protection in Supabase Auth with the Turnstile secret. Anonymous sign-in and admin password sign-in then send a one-time token; a challenge is shown only if Cloudflare asks for one. Without the key, sign-in works without a challenge, which leaves bot sign-ups open. Not tested against the real Turnstile service.
- **Legal pages.** Drafts live in `apps/web/content/legal/terms.md` and `privacy.md`. They publish at `/legal/terms` and `/legal/privacy`, appear in Settings, and are mentioned in onboarding. Have a lawyer review them and replace the operator contact before you treat them as binding.

## Automated image scanning (0017)
With `SCAN_PROVIDER=openai` and an `OPENAI_API_KEY`, the media Worker sends each upload to OpenAI's moderation model before storing it. A child-safety category (or a score of 0.2 or more) rejects the upload outright, stores nothing, and logs an urgent event for moderators (admin > Image scans). Other concerns (any score at or above `SCAN_REVIEW_THRESHOLD`, default 0.5) hold the post in the review queue. If the scanner is down the image is recorded as unscanned; with `media_scan_required = 1` such posts are held. **This is a general classifier, not hash matching of known abuse images, and it will miss things. It does not replace Cloudflare's CSAM Scanning Tool or human review.** Images are sent to OpenAI: disclose that in your Privacy Policy. Tested against a stand-in; the real API's response format and thresholds need checking in practice.
