# Introducing Inkognito: Speak Freely. Live Anonymously.

> **"Say what you can't say anywhere else."**

Modern social media turned every thought into personal branding. Follower counts, permanent profiles, indexed search histories, and algorithmic judgment created an internet where people curate who they appear to be rather than expressing who they actually are.

**Inkognito is the antidote.**

Inkognito is a next-generation anonymous social platform engineered from the ground up around radical privacy, clean design, and psychological freedom. No phone numbers. No emails. No follower counts. No permanent public profiles following you across the web. Just raw, authentic human thoughts and real conversations.

---

## 🌟 The Inkognito Philosophy: Private by Design

### 1. No Accounts, No Passwords, No Tracking
You don't register with an email address or connect a phone number. The moment you open Inkognito, an anonymous, cryptographically secured identity is established directly on your device.

### 2. Contextual Anonymity (Thread-Shifting Pseudonyms)
Most platforms track a single username everywhere you post, allowing anyone to piece together your identity. On Inkognito, **your identity changes depending on where you speak**.
- When you comment on a confession, you receive a unique generated pseudonym for that specific thread (e.g. *Silent Drifter #42*).
- When you participate in another thread across the platform, you receive a completely different pseudonym.
- Nobody can follow you from thread to thread or profile your history across the network.

### 3. Ephemeral & Disappearing Content
Some thoughts aren't meant to live on the internet forever:
- **Fading Posts**: Set your confession or comment to automatically disappear after a specified duration (hours or days).
- **24-Hour Whispers**: Fleeting micro-messages that naturally evaporate after 24 hours.
- **Auto-Purged Media**: Unused or expired image attachments are automatically scrubbed from storage.

### 📱 6. Multi-Device Sync & Recovery
Anonymity doesn't have to mean losing your account if you drop your phone in water:
- **Instant QR Pairing**: Scan a secure QR code from your phone to instantly link your tablet or desktop.
- **Cryptographic Recovery Code**: Generate a 25-character high-entropy recovery code (`XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX`). Store it in your password manager to restore your identity on any device.
- **Device Management**: View all active authorized devices and revoke old ones with a single tap.
- **Total Data Purge**: Choose to wipe all your posts and comments with one click, or completely vaporize your entire identity forever.

## 🛡️ Community Safety & Content Guardrails

Anonymity protects free speech, but it is never an excuse for abuse or harm. Inkognito implements rigorous safety boundaries:
1. **Zero Tolerance for Harm**: Strictly prohibited: exploitation of minors, harassment, blackmail, doxxing, non-consensual imagery, and threats of violence.
2. **Community-Driven Moderation**: Single-tap anonymous reporting with immediate triage for urgent safety concerns.
3. **Automated Image Scanning**: Incoming uploads are screened with computer vision safety classifiers before displaying.
4. **Silent Blocking**: Block any user instantly. You will never see their content again, and they are never notified of the block.
5. **Role-Gated Admin Control Room**: Human moderators work under strict MFA authentication and immutable audit logs to protect the health of the community without violating user privacy.
